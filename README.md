# groundtruth

**Anyone can claim it. Not everyone can prove it.**

Groundtruth is a hiring tool for engineering teams. It reads a candidate's GitHub, checks what is actually real, and gives you a ranked report for every role, where each line traces back to evidence.

Hire on verified work, not resumes.

🌐 **Website:** [ground-truth.vercel.app](https://ground-truth.vercel.app)

---

## The problem

Post a developer role today and the applications flood in. Most are polished by the same AI tools, list the same buzzwords, and read almost identically. You are not short on applicants. You are short on a way to know which ones are real.

Resumes are claims. Groundtruth checks them.

## How it works

1. **Create a job.** Set the role, the stack you need and where candidates can be based. Groundtruth gives you an apply link to post anywhere: LinkedIn, X or your own careers page.
2. **Candidates apply with GitHub.** No resume upload. Candidates sign in with GitHub, so the account is provably theirs, and leave an email for outreach.
3. **Every applicant is screened automatically.** The moment someone applies, their GitHub is read and scored against your specific role.
4. **Find your shortlist.** See every candidate ranked with evidence. Filter and sort by score, stack, country or experience.
5. **Reach out or export.** Draft personal outreach from a candidate's evidence, or export the full list to take it wherever you work.

## What gets checked

- Whether repositories are real work, not forks or abandoned pushes
- Commit activity and consistency over time
- Live deployments and releases
- Merged pull requests to third-party open-source projects

## How candidates are scored

Each candidate gets a score from **0 to 10 per job**, not one score for everyone. The same evidence can score differently for a backend role and a UI role, and the report explains why.

The score combines four things:

| Signal | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Stack match** | How closely their work overlaps with the stack you need |
| **Evidence strength** | How strong and verifiable their projects are |
| **Contributions** | Merged pull requests to external open-source projects |
| **AI review** | An AI reviewer's overall read of their profile and code |

## Principles

- **We read, not guess.** Repos, commit history and live projects are checked directly.
- **Facts stay separate from opinions.** What we found and what we think about it are shown in different places, so you can always tell which is which.
- **Every line has a receipt.** No claim in a report exists without a pointer to where it came from.
- **Unverifiable means unscored.** Private repositories cannot be verified, so they never move a score up or down. They are shown for transparency only.
- **Dead links are flagged, not hidden.**

## What groundtruth is not

It does not replace interviews. It replaces the time you spend guessing whether a resume deserves a reply. What you do with a strong report, such as an interview, a call or a trial project, is still up to you.

## Who it is for

Engineering teams, hiring managers and technical recruiters who receive many similar-looking applications and need a fast, honest way to see which candidates have real work behind them.
